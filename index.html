    Method

    • The watchlist is transcribed from U.S. Treasury and State Department releases. Every IMO number was checked against its check digit.
    • A listener connects to the free aisstream.io AIS feed for about 25 minutes every two hours. It recognises a watchlist vessel by the IMO number in its broadcast, then follows that vessel's position reports.
    • Because the listener runs about a fifth of the time, short gaps in a track belong to the listener. Only silences longer than 24 hours are drawn as dashed lines.
    • Signs worth logging by hand: a deeper draught after a silent stretch (loaded), two tankers side by side at sea, a new name or flag, and a radio identity whose country prefix does not match the claimed flag.
